# NextSlot · "11 pm" — Tabletop App Flythrough treatment

30 s · vertical 1080×1920 · 30 fps · American narrator with on-screen subtitles · one take.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One booking, card to card.** A new client sticker pops up on the studio's booking page at 11 pm, picks a service, takes a slot, pays the deposit; the deposit runs across the desk to the owner's phone, a bell flies over with the "New Booking" email, and the booking drops into Saturday on the calendar card. Pull back over the whole desk.
2. **Two halves of the desk.** Left: a chat card buried in message bubbles, a paper diary, a "proof of payment?" slip; right: the same booking flowing through NextSlot. Slow against fast.
3. **A countdown.** A timer card counts thirty seconds while one booking completes before it runs out.

**Chosen: 1.** It is the style's native shape (one item travels and changes at each stop), it shows the real product in order, and 30 s has room for one clean journey, not a comparison. Structure 2 needs twice the stations for half the screen time; 3 makes the timer, not the client, the subject.

## 2. Logline and arc

At 11 pm a new client books, holds and pays for a Saturday slot on a studio's NextSlot page, and by the time the camera pulls back the owner's calendar is filled and paid, without anyone typing a message.

- **Setup (0–5 s):** night on the desk; a client sticker pops up on the booking page.
- **Turn (5–19 s):** service → time (held) → deposit paid; the payment runs to the owner's phone and a bell delivers the booking.
- **Ending (19–30 s):** the booking lands in Saturday; pull back over the whole desk; the title card drops: NextSlot, start free.

The ending echoes the opening: the same client sticker, first standing alone on an empty page, finally lies printed into a full calendar.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** one-take software launch tours where interface windows lie flat as places (spatial continuity, a guide character carrying the action, the closing pull-back that shows the whole system); macro toy photography for low warm light and long soft shadows.
- **Don't take:** any app's real interface, icons, logos or characters; the library demo's story, cast, camera path or score.

## 6. Cue map

- 120 BPM, 4/4, 15 bars. Every hop, pop, stamp and tap on the 16th grid; pops and stamps on downbeats.
- Bars 1–2: vibraphone and soft pad only (night). Bar 2 downbeat: the pop, marimba enters.
- Bars 3–6: marimba ostinato, plucked bass, shaker; ducked under the voice.
- Bar 6 beat 2 (10.5 s): the stamp; a near-silence on the half beat before it (the voice line ends, the bed drops out).
- Bars 6–8: claps join for the data run and the bell.
- Bars 9–11: the calendar landing; the melody resolves.
- Bars 12–15: the full mallet theme in the gap after the last line; final chord to the last half-second.

The two near-silences: before the stamp (10.15–10.5) and before the title card drop (22.5–23.0).

## 8. Type and subtitles

- In the world, keywords and numbers only: service name and price, "10:30 · HELD", "PAY R225 DEPOSIT", "NEW BOOKING · DEPOSIT RECEIVED", "SAT 10:30 · PAID", the title card.
- Fonts: Montserrat 800 for pills, titles and subtitles; Inter for running print and spaced-caps labels (both OFL, bundled).
- Subtitles: one cue per spoken line, English only, in the style's quiet ink bar at the bottom; held at least 1.8 s and the line + 0.6 s.
- Brand: NextSlot bronze is the accent; cards print in the brand's ink and cream; the gear-clock icon and two-tone wordmark appear on the title card.

## 9. Claims

Every step is a shipped feature: the booking page, the 10-minute slot hold, deposits (PayShap on Starter, cards on Flow and up), the owner's "New Booking - Deposit received" email, Google Calendar sync. "Glow Studio" and its client are fictional.
