# Competitor positioning: video and online consultations

Research date: 2026-11-15. Method: web search only. Every vendor domain I tried to fetch (help.calendly.com, simplybook.me, www.timify.com, www.setmore.com, acuityscheduling.com) failed DNS resolution in this session, so all vendor claims below come from search-result snippets, not full page reads. Search results carried no publication dates, so each source is dated only by the retrieval date (2026-11-15) unless stated. Pricing was not verified for any vendor. Treat every feature claim as vendor-stated until someone reads the live page.

## Which platforms host video natively versus integrate

### Takeaway
Most of the listed platforms are integrators: they generate a Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams link and leave the call to that provider. Native or built-in video is offered by SimplyBook.me (Gruveo, plus Zoom and Teams links), Setmore (Teleport), Timify (MeetMe, a private app that must be requested from support), and Vagaro (a telehealth tool, positioned for clinical use). Calendly, Acuity, and Fresha are integration-only as far as the sources show.

### Cited Findings
- Calendly: native integrations with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, GoTo Meeting, and Webex; Zoom has been free on all plans since 2020, and Google Meet links appear in the calendar event and confirmation email. Caveat: round-robin and collective events need each team member to connect their own Google Meet account. Source: [Calendly Help Center](https://help.calendly.com/hc/en-us/articles/223195508) (search snippet only); [Calendly blog, integrations free forever](https://calendly.com/blog/meeting-integration-update) (search snippet only)
- Acuity: connects to Zoom through an Integrations panel. Acuity creates, reschedules, and removes Zoom meetings automatically. One video provider per calendar. Appointments booked before the integration existed get no link unless rebooked. Acuity's help says to use a different provider above 100 video appointments per day. Its own pages mention Zoom and Google Meet. Source: [Acuity Connect to Zoom](https://help.acuityscheduling.com/hc/articles/47748905289229-Connect-to-Zoom) (snippet); [Acuity video conferencing tutorial](https://acuityscheduling.com/learn/connect-video-conferencing) (snippet)
- SimplyBook.me: built-in online video meetings via Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Gruveo (its own video calling system); one page mentions Google Meet or other platforms. Zoom is one account per SimplyBook.me site. Teams lets team members connect their own accounts. Video and in-person sessions are separate services. Payment can be collected at booking. A 2020 help-centre post says Gruveo makes no HIPAA or GDPR claim. Source: [SimplyBook.me Start offering online](https://simplybook.me/en/book-online-video-meetings) (snippet); [SimplyBook.me Zoom help](https://help.simplybook.me/wiki/Online_Video_Meetings_via_Zoom) (snippet); [SimplyBook.me Gruveo post](https://news.simplybook.me/video-meetings-with-simplybook-me-gruveo-or-how-to-maintain-client-contact-in-times-of-crisis/) (snippet; the post's date was not shown, its "crisis" framing suggests 2020 but this is unconfirmed)
- Fresha: no video consultation feature found. Its health-practice pages describe in-person-style booking for consultations, with stored notes, intake forms, and patient histories. The only video mention found was a freelancer's Fiverr gig and a competitor, Pabau, advertising built-in video. Source: [Fresha health practice](https://fresha.com/for-business/health-practice) (snippet); [Fresha scheduling](https://www.fresha.com/for-business/features/scheduling) (snippet)
- Vagaro: telehealth tool that sends patients an automated notification with a unique session link, usable from desktop or mobile. Telehealth is tied to scheduling, billing, and documentation, and the weight-loss clinic page says telehealth visits can be booked, paid for, and documented in Vagaro. Vagaro says its video platform is HIPAA-compliant; it shows SOC 2 and ISO 27001 badges, which I did not verify. A marketing article says sessions can be saved for later reference. Source: [Vagaro medical weight loss clinic software](https://www.vagaro.com/pro/weight-loss-clinic-software) (snippet); [Vagaro teletherapy article](https://as-marketing-wus2.azurewebsites.net/learn/how-to-add-teletherapy-to-your-practice) (snippet, staging-looking domain, low confidence)
- Setmore: three video options. Google Meet is free for all users. Zoom is one-way, with a limit of 100 create/update requests per Zoom User ID per day. Teleport is built-in, free-tier, up to 4 participants, with lockable meetings. Video services are flagged per service, and links are created on booking. Setmore also offers video appointments via its AnswerConnect phone service. Source: [Setmore Google Meet support](https://support.setmore.com/en/articles/5271137-google-meet) (snippet); [Setmore Zoom support](https://support.setmore.com/en/articles/1412338-Zoom) (snippet); [Setmore Video call scheduling](https://www.setmore.com/schedule-virtual-meetings) (snippet)
- Timify: turns an existing service into a virtual one. Built-in video is MeetMe, which runs in the browser, but it is a private app you must request from Timify support before installing. Zoom and Teams links are generated automatically. Zoom and MeetMe apps are listed as Enterprise-tier per the snippet. GoToMeeting sync keeps the link unchanged on reschedule. Source: [Timify video appointment scheduling](https://www.timify.com/en/solutions/video-appointment-scheduling/) (snippet); [Timify MeetMe marketplace](https://www.timify.com/da/marketplace/meet-me) (snippet)

### Inferences
- A "built-in" video claim is not the same thing across vendors. SimplyBook.me (Gruveo), Setmore (Teleport), and Timify (MeetMe, gated) offer native rooms, but they differ in access and limits. Vagaro's telehealth tool is native but only described in clinical terms.
- Calendly, Acuity, and Fresha appear to rely on third-party meeting links for virtual visits.

### Gaps
- No vendor page was read in full, so exact plan gating for Zoom, Teams, and Meet is unconfirmed for Calendly, Acuity, and SimplyBook.me.
- Whether Calendly, Acuity, or Fresha host any native video was not verified against a live page.

## Headline and feature-page language

### Takeaway
The sources give only fragments of headline copy. What they show: Timify uses "Video appointment scheduling" as its solution-page title, and Setmore uses "Video call scheduling done right." Vagaro frames telehealth in clinical language. SimplyBook.me frames it as "Start offering online." I did not capture headline copy for Calendly, Acuity, or Fresha.

### Cited Findings
- Timify solution page title: "VIDEO APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING"; its marketplace copy describes "Automate virtual appointment scheduling with TIMIFY's own video solution, MeetMe". Source: [Timify solution](https://www.timify.com/en/solutions/video-appointment-scheduling/) (snippet); [Timify MeetMe](https://www.timify.com/da/marketplace/meet-me) (snippet)
- Setmore page title: "Video call scheduling done right." Source: [Setmore](https://www.setmore.com/schedule-virtual-meetings) (snippet)
- SimplyBook.me page title: "Start offering online." Source: [SimplyBook.me](https://simplybook.me/en/book-online-video-meetings) (snippet)
- Vagaro: "Vagaro integrates a robust, HIPAA-compliant telehealth platform that provides secure and seamless video sessions." Source: [Vagaro teletherapy article](https://as-marketing-wus2.azurewebsites.net/learn/how-to-add-teletherapy-to-your-practice) (snippet)
- Acuity blog title: "4 Steps to Book Virtual Consultations That Impress Clients." Source: [Acuity virtual consultations guide](https://it.acuityscheduling.com/learn/how-to-book-virtual-consultations) (snippet)
- Timify: "Turn your group sessions and bookings into video conferencing with TIMIFY." Source: [Timify blog](https://blog.timify.com/video-conferencing-scheduling/) (snippet)

### Inferences
- The "virtual consultation" and "video appointment" phrasing dominate; "online consultation" is less common in the headlines I saw.
- Vagaro's clinical HIPAA framing and Acuity's "impress clients" framing suggest two different buyer angles: compliance versus client experience.

### Gaps
- No Calendly or Fresha headline copy was captured.
- Headline text came from search-result titles, not from full-page reads.

## Client contact and payment for online appointments

### Takeaway
Payment at booking is confirmed for SimplyBook.me and Vagaro. Client contact is handled through automatic confirmation and reminder emails and SMS, which Timify states explicitly. Setmore and Acuity send links through booking confirmations, and Calendly puts links in the event and confirmation email. Payment for online sessions was not confirmed in full for the others.

### Cited Findings
- SimplyBook.me: payment can be collected at booking. Source: [SimplyBook.me](https://simplybook.me/en/book-online-video-meetings) (snippet)
- Vagaro: telehealth visits can be booked, paid for, and documented within Vagaro; automated notifications carry the session link. Source: [Vagaro weight-loss clinic](https://www.vagaro.com/pro/weight-loss-clinic-software) (snippet)
- Timify: confirmations and reminders go to all parties by email and/or SMS, and the Zoom app sends one or two reminders. Source: [Timify](https://www.timify.com/en/solutions/video-appointment-scheduling/) (snippet)
- Setmore: meeting links appear in confirmations and reminders. Source: [Setmore Google Meet](https://support.setmore.com/en/articles/5271137-google-meet) (snippet)
- Calendly: Google Meet link appears in the event and confirmation email. Source: [Calendly Help](https://help.calendly.com/hc/en-us/articles/223195508) (snippet)
- Bookem (South Africa): automatic reminders include a one-click join link; supports SnapScan, PayFast, and PayPal. Source: [Bookem doctors](https://bookem.com/en/doctors) (snippet, vendor-stated)

### Inferences
- Video links are typically bundled into the same confirmation and reminder flow as in-person appointments, which means the "online" message is mostly about the link, not a separate client journey.

### Gaps
- Whether Fresha, Vagaro, or SimplyBook.me take payment for virtual visits in the same way as in-person was not confirmed beyond the snippets above.
- No vendor's SMS or WhatsApp handling for join links was verified.

## South Africa and African-market tools

### Takeaway
Local tools lean into video consultations in a healthcare framing, and several add local payment rails. Bookem is the clearest Cape Town-based option. Most South African evidence comes from vendor pages and directory listings, so treat features as unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Bookem: developed, supported, and based in Cape Town. Video calls included with unlimited minutes in the subscription. Supports SnapScan, PayFast, and PayPal. Source: [Bookem doctors](https://bookem.com/en/doctors) (snippet); [Bookem GP](https://bookem.com/professions-gp) (snippet)
- SEDCO Teleconsultation: appointment booking, online payment, e-tickets, notifications, virtual waiting rooms, and video consultations. Source: [Capterra South Africa, SEDCO](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/1077457/SEDCO-Teleconsultation) (snippet)
- TeliMeds: custom booking sites, online scheduling, video consultations, e-prescribing, documentation. Source: [Capterra South Africa, TeliMeds](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/1077070/TeliMeds) (snippet)
- HasHealth: virtual and in-clinic appointments with HD video from any device. Source: [GetApp South Africa, HasHealth](https://www.getapp.za.com/software/2048966/hashealth) (snippet)
- Doccure: audio and video consultations alongside online appointment management. Source: [GetApp South Africa, Doccure](https://www.getapp.za.com/software/2057158/doccure) (snippet)
- Others surfaced but not checked: Vectera ("secure video meetings"), ExpertBox, and Doctolib and CLICKDOC (a GetApp comparison; CLICKDOC rated 1.9/5 by 10 users). Sources: [Capterra South Africa, ExpertBox](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/209171/expertbox) (snippet); [GetApp Doctolib vs CLICKDOC](https://www.getapp.za.com/compare/117267/141163/doctolib/vs/clickdoc) (snippet)

### Inferences
- The South African healthcare-booking tools combine video with e-prescribing or documentation, which is a different angle from the generic scheduler positioning of Calendly or Acuity.
- Local payment rails (SnapScan, PayFast) appear in the one local booking tool I could confirm.

### Gaps
- POPIA compliance claims for these tools were not verified.
- Pricing for all South African tools was not found.
- No South African beauty or salon booking tool with video was found in these results.

No pricing was verified for any vendor. The only price signals are third-party or unclear.

### Cited Findings

### Gaps
- Plan gating for Acuity, Fresha, Vagaro, and SimplyBook.me video features was not confirmed.

## Messaging angles seen

- Compliance: Vagaro (HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001 badges), Bookem and HasHealth (healthcare framing).
- Ease and no-download: Timify MeetMe ("nothing to download"), Setmore Teleport (free, up to 4 participants).
- Automation: Acuity and Timify (links created, moved, and removed automatically).
- Lock-in and integration: Vagaro (telehealth tied to scheduling, billing, documentation); Timify (Zoom, Teams, GoToMeeting sync).
- Client experience: Acuity ("impress clients"), Setmore ("done right").

## Caveats
- Dates: search results were undated. Retrieval date 2026-11-15 is the only date I can assert.
- Verification: no vendor domain resolved for full-page fetch in this session. Re-run with those domains allowed (help.calendly.com, simplybook.me, www.timify.com, www.setmore.com, acuityscheduling.com) to confirm claims and pricing.
- Some results were on staging or regional mirror domains (for example, stg.timify.com, the Vagaro azurewebsites marketing host). I treated those as low confidence.
- Several Capterra and GetApp listings are directory data, not vendor claims.
